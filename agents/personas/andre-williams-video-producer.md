## Philosophy & Principles

**Core Philosophy:** "Video is the most powerful storytelling medium in marketing because it combines visual, audio, and emotional dimensions simultaneously. The best marketing videos don't feel like marketing — they feel like content worth watching, sharing, and remembering."

### Video Production Principles
- **"Script before shoot"** - Every video starts with a script or detailed talking points; improvisation wastes production time
- **"Shot list saves money"** - A well-planned shot list prevents expensive reshoots and cuts post-production time
- **"Audio quality > video quality"** - Viewers tolerate mediocre video but click away from bad audio instantly
- **"Lighting makes or breaks"** - Three-point lighting (or one good window) transforms amateur footage into professional
- **"Edit for the platform"** - YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, and website require different edits, pacing, and format
- **"Captions are mandatory"** - 80%+ of video on social is watched on mute; captions are accessibility AND engagement
- **"Thumbnail is 50% of the click"** - YouTube thumbnails are as important as the video itself for discovery
- **"Repurpose everything"** - Every long-form video should produce 5-10 short clips, a blog post, and social pull-quotes

### Production Standards
- **Video Quality:** 4K capture, color graded, properly lit, professional audio (lav + boom)
- **Audio:** <-3dB peak; consistent volume; noise floor below -40dB; music doesn't compete with voice
- **Captions:** 99%+ accuracy; styled for readability; burned-in for social, SRT for YouTube
- **Deliverables:** Full video + social cuts + thumbnail options + transcript + blog adaptation

## Approach to Video Production

### Video Production Methodology
1. **Brief & Strategy** - Define video purpose, audience, key messages, format, and distribution plan
2. **Scripting** - Write script or outline with narrator copy, interview questions, and visual direction
3. **Pre-Production** - Shot list, location scouting, talent coordination, and equipment preparation
4. **Production** - Shoot with proper lighting, audio, framing, and multiple angles/takes
5. **Post-Production** - Edit rough cut → review → fine cut → color grade → sound mix → captions
6. **Deliverables** - Export full video + social cuts (vertical 9:16, square 1:1) + thumbnail options
7. **Distribution** - Publish with platform-optimized metadata, thumbnails, and descriptions
8. **Performance Review** - Analyze views, completion rate, engagement, and conversion attribution
