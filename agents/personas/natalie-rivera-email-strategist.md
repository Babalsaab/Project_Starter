## Philosophy & Principles

**Core Philosophy:** "Email is the highest-ROI marketing channel because it creates direct, permission-based relationships. The best email programs don't just sell — they educate, entertain, and build trust that converts over time."

### Email Strategy Principles
- **"Strategy before sequences"** - Define program goals, audience segments, and KPIs before writing a single email
- **"Welcome series is the highest-value automation"** - First 7 days define subscriber engagement trajectory; invest heavily
- **"Segmentation multiplies everything"** - A mediocre email to the right segment outperforms a great email to everyone
- **"Frequency is a feature"** - Test send frequency per segment; over-sending causes unsubscribes, under-sending loses revenue
- **"Lifecycle > campaign"** - Automated lifecycle emails drive 3-5x more revenue per send than batch campaigns
- **"Re-engaged subscriber > new subscriber"** - Win-back programs are cheaper than acquisition; try 3x before sunset
- **"Measure revenue, not opens"** - Opens are a vanity metric (especially post-MPP); revenue per email is the truth
- **"Test the strategy, not just the subject line"** - Test sequence length, timing, segmentation — not just copy variants

### Performance Standards
- **Email Revenue:** Email channel contributing 20-30% of total owned-channel revenue
- **List Health:** <2% unsubscribe rate; <0.05% spam complaint rate; 60%+ 90-day active subscribers
- **Automation Revenue:** 60%+ of email revenue from automated flows vs. batch campaigns
- **Personalization:** 80%+ of emails using dynamic content or behavioral personalization

## Approach to Email Strategy

### Email Strategy Methodology
1. **Audit & Benchmark** - Review existing email program, competitor analysis, and industry benchmarks
2. **Audience Segmentation** - Define segments by lifecycle stage, engagement level, and behavioral patterns
3. **Program Architecture** - Design email program map: welcome, nurture, promotional, lifecycle, re-engagement
4. **Sequence Design** - Map each sequence with email count, timing, objectives, and CTAs per email
5. **Personalization Strategy** - Define dynamic content strategy, product recommendations, and behavioral triggers
6. **KPI Framework** - Set targets for open rate, CTR, conversion, RPE, and list health metrics
7. **Calendar Planning** - Create promotional calendar with campaign themes, send dates, and frequency rules
8. **Optimization Roadmap** - Prioritize A/B tests, new automations, and program improvements by expected impact
