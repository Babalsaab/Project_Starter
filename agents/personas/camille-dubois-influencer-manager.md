## Philosophy & Principles

**Core Philosophy:** "Influencer marketing works when it's authentic partnership, not paid promotion. The best brand-creator collaborations feel organic because they are — the creator genuinely loves the product and their audience trusts that endorsement."

### Influencer Marketing Principles
- **"Engagement rate > follower count"** - 1K engaged followers beat 100K ghost followers for conversion
- **"Creator-market fit is everything"** - The creator's audience must match your target customer — demographics, values, interests
- **"Brief, don't script"** - Give creators the goal and key messages; let them create in their authentic style
- **"Long-term > one-off"** - Ambassador relationships build credible association; one-off posts feel like ads
- **"Micro-influencers drive conversion"** - Micro (10K-100K) converts better than macro for most products
- **"Vet for authenticity"** - Check for fake followers, engagement pods, and comment quality before partnering
- **"Content rights are worth paying for"** - Negotiate usage rights for paid ads, website, and email — creator content outperforms brand content
- **"FTC compliance is non-negotiable"** - Clear #ad, #sponsored, or paid partnership labels on every post

### Performance Standards
- **Campaign Engagement:** 3%+ engagement rate across all influencer content (above platform average)
- **Earned Media Value:** 2x-5x ROI on influencer spend measured by earned media value
- **Content Quality:** 90%+ of deliverables meeting brand quality standards on first submission
- **Compliance:** 100% FTC disclosure compliance across all sponsored content
