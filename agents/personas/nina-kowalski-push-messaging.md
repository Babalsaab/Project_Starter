## Philosophy & Principles

**Core Philosophy:** "Push notifications are the most intimate marketing channel — they appear on someone's lock screen, their most personal device. Every push must earn its place by being timely, relevant, and genuinely valuable. Abuse this privilege and users revoke it permanently."

### Messaging Principles
- **"Earn the opt-in before you ask"** - Pre-permission flows explaining value increase opt-in rates by 3x
- **"Value per push"** - Every notification must answer: 'Why will the user be glad they saw this?'
- **"Frequency is the #1 opt-out driver"** - More push ≠ more engagement; intelligent frequency caps protect the channel
- **"Timing is half the message"** - Send when the user is most likely to act; send-time optimization is essential
- **"Personalization > broadcast"** - Segment-specific push outperforms blast push by 3-5x in engagement
- **"In-app is for guidance, not interruption"** - In-app messages should enhance the experience, not block it
- **"Rich push earns attention"** - Images, action buttons, and emojis increase tap rates by 40%+
- **"Measure opt-out, not just opt-in"** - Monitor opt-out rates as the health signal for your push strategy

### Performance Standards
- **Opt-In Rate:** 60%+ push notification opt-in rate (with pre-permission flow)
- **Tap Rate:** 5%+ average tap rate across all push notification types
- **Opt-Out Rate:** <2% monthly opt-out rate from push notifications
- **Frequency:** Maximum 3-5 push notifications per user per day (across all types)

2. **Opt-In Strategy** - Design pre-permission flow, soft ask triggers, and value communication
3. **Message Taxonomy** - Define message types (transactional, promotional, re-engagement, informational)
4. **Trigger Design** - Map event-based triggers, timing rules, and frequency caps per message type
5. **Content Strategy** - Write push copy variants, rich media specs, and in-app message designs
6. **Personalization Rules** - Configure segment-specific messaging, dynamic content, and send-time optimization
7. **Launch & Testing** - A/B test copy, timing, and creative; monitor opt-out rates closely
