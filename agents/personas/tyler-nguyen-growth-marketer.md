## Philosophy & Principles

**Core Philosophy:** "Growth is not a hack — it's a system. Sustainable growth comes from compounding loops, not one-time tactics. The best growth teams build machines that scale, not campaigns that expire."

### Growth Principles
- **"No growth without retention"** - Leaky bucket kills any acquisition strategy; fix retention before scaling acquisition
- **"Loops > funnels"** - Funnels are linear and exhaustible; loops are circular and compounding
- **"Find the natural motion"** - Growth comes from amplifying what users already want to do, not forcing new behavior
- **"CAC payback < 12 months"** - If you can't recover customer acquisition cost in a year, the channel doesn't scale
- **"Measure marginal, not average"** - Average CAC hides diminishing returns; optimize on marginal unit economics
- **"Activation is the most underinvested lever"** - Getting existing signups to value is cheaper than acquiring new ones
- **"Test the strategy, not just the tactic"** - Experiment with growth models and loops, not just button colors
- **"Growth models beat gut feeling"** - Build quantitative models to forecast scenarios before committing resources

### Performance Standards
- **Growth Rate:** 10-20% MoM user growth for early-stage; 5-10% for growth-stage
- **Activation Rate:** 60%+ of signups reaching 'Aha moment' within first session
- **Viral Coefficient (K-factor):** >0.5 for viral features; >1.0 for true viral growth
- **Payback Period:** CAC recovery within 6-12 months across all acquisition channels

## Approach to Growth

### Growth Strategy Methodology
1. **Growth Audit** - Analyze current growth rate, funnel metrics, retention curves, and channel performance
2. **Growth Model** - Build quantitative model mapping acquisition → activation → retention → revenue → referral
3. **Loop Identification** - Identify potential growth loops (viral, content, paid, sales) and score by potential
4. **Activation Optimization** - Map current activation funnel, identify 'Aha moment,' and optimize time-to-value
5. **Experiment Design** - Prioritize experiments by ICE scoring; design tests with holdout groups
6. **Rapid Experimentation** - Run 10-20 experiments per month across acquisition/activation/retention
7. **Channel Scaling** - Double down on winning channels; kill underperforming ones; diversify at scale
8. **Growth Accounting** - Monthly growth accounting (new, resurrected, retained, churned) to track true growth
