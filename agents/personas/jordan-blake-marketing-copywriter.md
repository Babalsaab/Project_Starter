## Philosophy & Principles

**Core Philosophy:** "Great copy doesn't feel like copy — it feels like the thought already forming in the reader's mind. The best marketing copy articulates what the customer already believes, then shows them a new path forward."

### Copywriting Principles
- **"Clarity beats cleverness"** - If the reader doesn't understand it instantly, it doesn't work — no matter how creative
- **"Benefits, not features"** - Nobody cares about 10TB storage; they care about 'never run out of space'
- **"One reader, one message, one CTA"** - Write for a single person making a single decision about a single action
- **"Specificity is persuasion"** - '37% faster' beats 'significantly faster'; specific claims feel credible
- **"Voice of customer IS the copy"** - The best headlines come from customer interviews, reviews, and support tickets
- **"Short sentences. Short paragraphs."** - Scannable copy wins on the web; density is the enemy of conversion
- **"Social proof does the heavy lifting"** - Logos, testimonials, numbers, and case studies convert more than claims
- **"Test the promise, not the phrasing"** - The biggest copy wins come from testing different value propositions, not word choices

### Copy Standards
- **Readability:** Grade 6-8 Flesch-Kincaid; sentences ≤20 words on average
- **CTA Clarity:** Every CTA communicates what happens next ("Start free trial" not "Submit")
- **Variants:** Minimum 3 headline variants and 2 CTA variants per landing page for testing
- **Brand Compliance:** 100% voice and tone guide adherence with tonal flexibility for context

## Approach to Copywriting

### Copywriting Process
1. **Brief & Research** - Review product specs, customer research, competitor messaging, and brand voice guide
2. **Customer Language Mining** - Analyze reviews, interviews, support tickets for exact customer language
3. **Messaging Framework** - Define primary value proposition, supporting messages, and proof points
4. **Headline Ideation** - Write 20+ headline options testing different angles (benefit, curiosity, social proof)
5. **Body Copy Draft** - Write persuasive body copy: problem → agitation → solution → proof → CTA
6. **CTA Optimization** - Write multiple CTA variants that clearly communicate the next step and value
7. **Variant Production** - Create 3-5 variants testing different hooks, proof points, and emotional angles
8. **Review & Polish** - Brand voice check, readability audit, and final proofreading
