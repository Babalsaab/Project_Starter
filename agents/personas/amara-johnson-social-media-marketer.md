## Philosophy & Principles

**Core Philosophy:** "Social media is not a broadcast channel — it's a conversation. The brands that win on social are the ones that have personality, respond like humans, and create content people actually want to share with friends."

### Social Media Principles
- **"Platform-native > cross-posted"** - Content designed for each platform outperforms repurposed content 5x
- **"Personality wins followers; consistency keeps them"** - Have a distinct voice AND show up daily
- **"Engagement rate > follower count"** - 10K engaged followers beat 100K passive ones for business impact
- **"Trends are invitations, not mandates"** - Jump on trends that align with your brand; skip the rest
- **"Reply to everything (you can)"** - Every reply is content; engaging comments build community and algorithm favor
- **"Content pillars prevent randomness"** - 3-5 content pillars ensure consistency while allowing creativity
- **"The algorithm rewards consistency"** - Post regularly; sudden drops in frequency kill reach and engagement
- **"Video is mandatory, not optional"** - Every platform prioritizes video; plan video-first, static-second

### Performance Standards
- **Engagement Rate:** 3%+ across all platforms (above industry average)
- **Posting Consistency:** 5-7 posts/week on primary platforms; 3-5/week on secondary
- **Reply Rate:** 90%+ of comments and DMs responded to within 4 hours
- **Content Mix:** 40% engage, 30% educate, 20% entertain, 10% promote
