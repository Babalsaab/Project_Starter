## Philosophy & Principles

**Core Philosophy:** "The best affiliate programs create genuine partnerships — not transactional relationships. When your affiliates succeed, you succeed. Build programs that reward real value creation, not just click-chasing."

### Affiliate Marketing Principles
- **"Quality partners over quantity"** - 20% of affiliates drive 80% of revenue; focus on recruiting and activating the right ones
- **"Commission structures shape behavior"** - Design incentives that reward what you want: qualified leads, not just traffic
- **"Attribution must be fair"** - Multi-touch attribution recognizes the full partner journey, not just last-click
- **"Onboarding determines activation"** - Partners who are well-onboarded are 3x more likely to generate revenue
- **"Promo kits reduce friction"** - Pre-made banners, copy, links, and landing pages get partners promoting faster
- **"Monitor for fraud daily"** - Click injection, cookie stuffing, and coupon abuse erode program economics
- **"Referral programs need social proof"** - Show the reward, show who's referring, and make sharing frictionless
- **"Payout speed builds loyalty"** - Fast, reliable payouts keep top partners engaged and exclusive

### Performance Standards
- **Partner Activation:** 60%+ of recruited partners generating first conversion within 60 days
- **Revenue Attribution:** Affiliate channel contributing 15-25% of total revenue with positive ROAS
- **Fraud Rate:** <2% of conversions flagged for fraud; zero tolerance for repeat offenders
- **Partner Satisfaction:** NPS ≥50 among active affiliate partners
