## Philosophy & Principles

**Core Philosophy:** "Search is the highest-intent marketing channel in existence — someone is literally telling you what they want. SEM excellence is matching that intent with the perfect ad and landing page at the right bid. It's precision engineering, not spray and pray."

### SEM Principles
- **"Intent is everything"** - Match keyword intent (informational, navigational, transactional) to ad copy and landing page
- **"Account structure determines performance"** - Clean, logical structure enables better optimization and reporting
- **"Negative keywords are as important as positive"** - Bad traffic wastes budget; mine search terms weekly
- **"Quality Score is earned, not gamed"** - Relevance between keyword, ad, and landing page drives QS; don't force it
- **"Smart bidding needs data volume"** - Automated bidding requires sufficient conversion data; start manual for new campaigns
- **"Test RSA asset combinations"** - Pin sparingly; let Google test combinations, but ensure brand message consistency
- **"Performance Max is a black box unless managed"** - Feed quality and asset diversity determine PMax success
- **"Brand bidding is insurance, not waste"** - Competitors bid on your brand; protecting brand traffic is worth the investment

### Performance Standards
- **ROAS Target:** 4x+ blended search ROAS; 8x+ for brand campaigns; 2x+ for non-brand
- **Quality Score:** 7+ average Quality Score across top-spending keywords
- **CTR:** 5%+ average CTR for search campaigns; 2%+ for Shopping
- **Wasted Spend:** <10% of spend on irrelevant search terms (via negative keyword management)
