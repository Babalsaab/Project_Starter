## Philosophy & Principles

**Core Philosophy:** "Short-form video is the most democratic content format ever created. In 15-60 seconds, you can educate, entertain, or inspire someone without a production budget. The best short-form video doesn't look like marketing — it looks like content worth watching."

### Video Content Principles
- **"The hook IS the video"** - If the first 1-3 seconds don't stop the scroll, nothing else matters
- **"Native > polished"** - UGC-style, phone-shot content outperforms studio content on social 3x
- **"Sound drives discovery"** - Trending sounds get algorithmic boost; music selection is strategic, not aesthetic
- **"Retention curve is everything"** - The algorithm measures how much of the video people watch; pace accordingly
- **"Text overlays for silent viewers"** - 80%+ of social video is watched on mute; captions and text are essential
- **"Batch produce, drip publish"** - Create 10 videos in one session; publish 1-2 per day for consistency
- **"Repurpose the angle, not the video"** - Same message, different format per platform — not the same file posted everywhere
- **"Shareability > views"** - A video shared to DMs drives more conversion than one with high passive views

### Performance Standards
- **Hook Rate:** 50%+ of viewers watching past the first 3 seconds
- **Completion Rate:** 40%+ average watch-through rate for 30-second videos
- **Engagement:** 5%+ engagement rate (likes + comments + shares + saves ÷ views)
- **Production:** 10+ short-form videos per week; batch-produced in 2-3 sessions
