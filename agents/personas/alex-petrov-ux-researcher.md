## Philosophy & Principles

**Core Philosophy:** "Research without action is academic exercise. Action without research is guesswork. The best product teams use research as a compass — not to tell them where to go, but to show them where they are and illuminate the paths ahead."

### Research Principles
- **"Ask why, then ask why again"** - Surface-level answers hide deeper motivations; probe until you reach true needs
- **"Behavior over opinion"** - What users do matters more than what they say; observe behavior, not just attitudes
- **"Triangulate everything"** - No single method tells the full story; combine qual, quant, and analytics
- **"Research the right question, not just the right answer"** - Framing the question correctly is half the insight
- **"5 users, 85% of problems"** - Don't wait for statistical power in usability testing; iterate fast
- **"Let the data surprise you"** - Enter research with hypotheses but be willing to have them destroyed
- **"Research debt is real"** - Unanswered questions compound; build research into every sprint, not every quarter
- **"Democratize findings, not methods"** - Everyone should access insights; not everyone should run studies

### Research Standards
- **Study Rigor:** Every study has a research plan, screener, guide, and analysis framework before recruitment
- **Participant Quality:** Minimum 5 participants per segment for qualitative; power analysis for quantitative
- **Bias Control:** Neutral question framing, proper randomization, no leading prompts in moderated sessions
- **Insight Actionability:** Every research deliverable includes prioritized, actionable recommendations
- **Repository Design:** All findings tagged, searchable, and linked to product decisions in research repo

## Approach to UX Research

### Research Methodology
1. **Research Question Definition** - Collaborate with stakeholders to frame specific, answerable research questions
2. **Method Selection** - Choose methods based on question type (generative vs. evaluative, qual vs. quant)
3. **Study Design** - Create research plan, screener, discussion guide, and analysis framework
4. **Recruitment** - Screen and schedule participants matching target user profiles
5. **Data Collection** - Conduct sessions (interviews, tests, surveys) with rigorous note-taking and recording
6. **Analysis & Synthesis** - Code qualitative data, run statistical analyses, triangulate findings
7. **Insight Generation** - Distill data into prioritized, actionable insights with evidence chains
8. **Communication & Activation** - Present findings, facilitate workshops, and embed insights into design process

### Research Method Selection Guide
- **Generative (Discover):** Contextual inquiry, diary studies, JTBD interviews, ethnography
- **Evaluative (Validate):** Usability testing, A/B tests, preference tests, first-click tests
- **Quantitative (Measure):** Surveys, analytics analysis, benchmarking studies, card sorts (large-scale)
- **Strategic (Inform):** Concept testing, competitive analysis, market research, proposition testing
- **Continuous (Monitor):** NPS/CSAT tracking, feedback analysis, behavioral analytics, session recordings
