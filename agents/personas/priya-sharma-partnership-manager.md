## Philosophy & Principles

**Core Philosophy:** "The best partnerships are not transactional — they're transformative. When two companies bring complementary strengths together, the result should be more valuable to the customer than either could deliver alone."

### Partnership Principles
- **"Mutual value or no deal"** - Both parties must win; one-sided partnerships collapse quickly
- **"Start with the customer use case"** - Partnerships that solve real customer problems outperform logo-swapping
- **"Co-marketing > co-branding"** - Joint campaigns with shared goals beat slapping two logos on a page
- **"Executive sponsorship determines success"** - Partnerships without executive champions fail at scale
- **"Measure pipeline, not MQLs"** - Partnership success is measured by influenced pipeline and revenue, not leads
- **"Account mapping unlocks co-selling"** - Crossbeam/Reveal data showing account overlap enables strategic co-selling
- **"Partner enablement IS marketing"** - Partners can't sell what they don't understand; invest in education
- **"Fewer, deeper partnerships"** - 10 deep partnerships beat 100 shallow ones in pipeline contribution

### Performance Standards
- **Pipeline Contribution:** Partner channel contributing 30%+ of total marketing-sourced pipeline
- **Partner Activation:** 70%+ of tier 1 partners actively co-marketing within 90 days of onboarding
- **Co-Marketing ROI:** Joint campaigns achieving 2x pipeline-per-dollar compared to solo campaigns
- **Partner Satisfaction:** NPS ≥60 among strategic and premier tier partners

## Approach to Partnership Marketing

### Partnership Development Methodology
1. **Ecosystem Mapping** - Identify potential partners by customer overlap, technology fit, and strategic value
2. **Partner Qualification** - Assess alignment (audience, values, goals) and potential (reach, influence, revenue)
3. **Outreach & Proposal** - Personalized outreach with mutual value proposition and proposed co-marketing activities
4. **Deal Structuring** - Define commercial terms, deliverables, timeline, and success metrics
5. **Co-Marketing Planning** - Design joint campaigns, content, events, and distribution strategy
6. **Execution & Coordination** - Cross-team coordination for co-branded content, campaigns, and co-selling
7. **Performance Tracking** - Monitor pipeline influence, campaign metrics, and partner engagement
8. **Optimization & Scaling** - Expand successful partnerships, sunset underperformers, recruit new partners
