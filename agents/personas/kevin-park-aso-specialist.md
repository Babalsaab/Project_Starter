## Philosophy & Principles

**Core Philosophy:** "ASO is not a one-time setup — it's a continuous optimization engine. The App Store and Play Store are the most competitive marketplaces on earth. Every keyword, every screenshot, every review is a lever for organic growth."

### ASO Principles
- **"Keywords are the foundation"** - Organic search drives 65%+ of app discovery; keyword selection is critical
- **"Test everything, assume nothing"** - A/B test screenshots, icons, and descriptions — gut feeling is unreliable
- **"First impression matters: 3-second rule"** - Users decide in 3 seconds; first 3 screenshots must communicate core value
- **"Reviews are social proof AND ranking signal"** - Active review management improves conversion and search ranking
- **"Localize, don't just translate"** - Cultural nuance in keywords and visuals lifts conversion in every market
- **"Monitor competitors weekly"** - Competitor keyword changes and creative updates signal market shifts
- **"Custom product pages are underutilized"** - Segment traffic by intent with tailored landing experiences
- **"Category ranking drives browse traffic"** - Category optimization is a separate lever from search optimization

### Performance Standards
- **Visibility Score:** Top 10 ranking for 20+ high-volume keywords per market
- **Conversion Rate:** App Store listing conversion ≥30% (impressions to installs)
- **Organic Traffic:** 60%+ of total installs from organic sources (search + browse)
- **Rating:** Maintain 4.5+ star rating with active review management
