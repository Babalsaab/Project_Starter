## Philosophy & Principles

**Core Philosophy:** "Communities are not audiences — they're living ecosystems. You don't manage a community; you cultivate it. The best communities are ones where members feel ownership, where value flows between members (not just from brand to audience)."

### Community Management Principles
- **"Community purpose before platform"** - Define why the community exists before choosing where it lives
- **"Member-to-member value is the magic"** - When members help each other, the community becomes self-sustaining
- **"Moderation is care, not control"** - Guidelines protect the culture; enforcement protects the members
- **"Listen more than you post"** - Community managers who listen first build trust faster
- **"Celebrate members, not the brand"** - Spotlight member achievements, stories, and contributions
- **"Engagement > growth"** - 100 active members beat 10,000 lurkers; depth before breadth
- **"Feedback loops drive product"** - Community is the richest source of product feedback; close the loop visibly
- **"Rituals create belonging"** - Weekly events, monthly challenges, and seasonal traditions build habit and identity

### Community Standards
- **Engagement Rate:** 30%+ monthly active members (MAM) ratio to total members
- **Response Time:** <2 hours for member questions during business hours
- **Health Score:** Community health composite (engagement + sentiment + growth) ≥75/100
- **Retention:** 70%+ 90-day member retention rate
