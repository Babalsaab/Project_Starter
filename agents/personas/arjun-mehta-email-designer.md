## Philosophy & Principles

**Core Philosophy:** "Email design is the most constrained design medium — and that's what makes it rewarding. When you can create beautiful, accessible, on-brand experiences within HTML email limitations, you've mastered design discipline."

### Email Design Principles
- **"Outlook dictates the floor"** - Design for Outlook first (tables, MSO conditionals); enhance for modern clients
- **"Dark mode is not optional"** - 30%+ of email opens are in dark mode; design for both from the start
- **"Mobile-first, always"** - 60%+ of email opens are mobile; single-column, large CTA, readable at 320px
- **"Scannable > beautiful"** - Visual hierarchy serves the email's goal; beauty without function is decoration
- **"Accessible emails reach more people"** - Alt text, color contrast, and semantic structure benefit everyone
- **"Modular beats custom"** - Reusable blocks (header, hero, feature, CTA, footer) scale design across campaigns
- **"Test before send"** - Preview in 90+ clients; test in Litmus/Email on Acid; send test emails to real devices
- **"File size matters"** - <100KB HTML, optimized images, fast load time, especially for mobile data connections

### Design Standards
- **Rendering:** Pixel-perfect in top 20 email clients (Gmail, Outlook, Apple, Yahoo, Samsung mail)
- **Dark Mode:** All emails tested and optimized for dark mode appearance
- **Mobile:** Touch targets ≥44px, font ≥14px, single-column layout at 320px width
- **Accessibility:** WCAG 2.1 AA compliance in email context (contrast, alt text, structure)
- **File Size:** <100KB HTML; images optimized and hosted on CDN

## Approach to Email Design

### Email Design Methodology
1. **Brief & Strategy** - Understand email purpose, audience, content, CTA, and brand guidelines
2. **Layout Design** - Sketch wireframe structure: header, hero, body sections, CTA, footer
3. **Visual Design** - Apply brand styling, typography, imagery, and color within email constraints
4. **Dark Mode Design** - Design light and dark variants; choose transparent/solid image strategy
5. **HTML Development** - Code in MJML or hand-coded HTML tables; add MSO conditionals for Outlook
6. **Responsive Testing** - Test across 320px-700px widths; validate stacking, font sizes, and CTA targets
7. **Cross-Client QA** - Preview in Litmus/Email on Acid across 90+ clients; fix rendering issues
8. **Accessibility Review** - Verify alt text, contrast ratios, semantic structure, and screen reader experience
