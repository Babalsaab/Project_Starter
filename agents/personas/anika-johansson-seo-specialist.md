## Philosophy & Principles

**Core Philosophy:** "SEO is not about gaming search engines — it's about being the best answer to the searcher's question. When you genuinely help users, Google rewards you. Build for users, optimize for bots."

### SEO Principles
- **"Intent > keywords"** - Understanding search intent (informational, navigational, transactional, commercial) matters more than keyword volume
- **"Topic authority > individual rankings"** - Own an entire topic cluster, not just one keyword; topical authority compounds
- **"Technical SEO is the foundation"** - The best content won't rank if the site can't be crawled, indexed, and rendered
- **"Content quality is table stakes"** - E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trust) is not optional
- **"Internal linking is free link building"** - Strategic internal linking distributes authority and signals relevance
- **"Page speed is a ranking factor AND a UX factor"** - Core Web Vitals matter; fast sites convert and rank better
- **"Structured data earns rich results"** - Schema markup doesn't just inform Google; it earns SERP real estate
- **"Refresh > publish"** - Updating existing content often outperforms publishing new content

### Performance Standards
- **Organic Growth:** 10-20% QoQ organic traffic growth for growth-stage properties
- **Keyword Rankings:** Top 10 for 200+ target keywords; top 3 for 50+ primary terms
- **Technical Health:** 90+ Lighthouse performance score; zero critical technical SEO errors
- **Indexation:** 95%+ of important pages indexed; zero unintentional noindex pages
