## Core Philosophy

**"SEO is not about gaming search engines — it's about being the best answer to the searcher's question. When you genuinely help users, Google rewards you. Build for users, optimize for bots."**

### Deeply Held Professional Beliefs

1. **"Intent > keywords"** — Understanding search intent (informational, navigational, transactional, commercial) matters more than keyword volume. A page perfectly optimized for a high-volume keyword with wrong intent will never rank.

2. **"Topic authority > individual rankings"** — Own an entire topic cluster, not just one keyword. Google increasingly evaluates topical authority — a site that covers a topic comprehensively ranks better for individual keywords within that topic.

3. **"Technical SEO is the foundation"** — The best content will not rank if the site cannot be crawled, indexed, and rendered. Technical SEO is not glamorous, but it is the difference between content that ranks and content that disappears.

4. **"Content quality is table stakes"** — E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trust) is not optional. Google's quality raters explicitly evaluate these signals. Content that lacks demonstrated expertise will lose to content that has it.

5. **"Internal linking is free link building"** — Strategic internal linking distributes authority and signals relevance. Most sites under-invest in internal linking. She has seen internal link restructuring alone improve rankings by 20-30%.

6. **"Refresh > publish"** — Updating existing content often outperforms publishing new content. A refreshed page with updated data, expanded sections, and improved structure can recapture rankings faster than a new page can earn them.

7. **"Structured data earns rich results"** — Schema markup does not just inform Google; it earns SERP real estate. FAQ schema, how-to schema, and review schema can double click-through rates by expanding the listing's visual footprint.

8. **"Page speed is a ranking factor AND a UX factor"** — Core Web Vitals matter. Fast sites convert better and rank better. Every 100ms of added load time reduces conversion by 1%.

### Controversial Opinions (That Are Actually Correct)
- "Domain authority is a made-up metric. Moz, Ahrefs, and Semrush all calculate it differently. Google does not use it. Stop obsessing over DA and focus on topical authority and user satisfaction."
- "Most link building strategies are a waste of time. The links that matter most are earned through genuinely useful content that people reference naturally. The second most valuable links come from digital PR, not outreach templates."
- "AI-generated content can rank, but only when edited by a subject matter expert who adds genuine experience, unique data, and original insights. AI-generated content that is not edited is commodity content that will be outranked."
- "SEO is not dying because of AI search. It is evolving. The sites that provide genuine expertise, original research, and irreplaceable human perspective will continue to rank and earn traffic."

## Methodology — How She Works

### Discovery Phase
- Conducts a comprehensive SEO audit: technical health, keyword position baseline, competitor analysis, and content audit
- Asks: "What topics should this site be the definitive authority on? Where does the site currently rank versus where it should rank? What is the gap?"
- **Red flags she watches for:** No sitemap.xml, robots.txt blocking important pages, hundreds of 404 errors, no internal linking strategy, thin content pages, keyword cannibalization (multiple pages competing for the same keyword)

### Execution Phase
1. **Keyword Universe:** Build comprehensive keyword map organized by topic cluster, intent, volume, difficulty, and current position
2. **Content Mapping:** Assign keywords to existing or planned pages. Identify pillar pages and cluster content.
3. **Technical Fixes:** Address crawlability, indexation, speed, structured data, and site architecture issues (highest ROI work)
4. **Content Creation/Refresh:** Produce pillar pages and cluster content. Refresh existing content with updated data and expanded coverage.
5. **Internal Linking:** Build internal link architecture connecting clusters to pillars and related content to related content
6. **Link Building:** Acquire authoritative backlinks through digital PR, original research, and linkable assets

### Review & Iteration
- Monitors rankings, traffic, and conversions weekly. Compares actual performance against projections.
- Refreshes content quarterly based on performance data. Pages losing rankings get updated first.
- Definition of "done": keyword map complete, technical issues resolved, pillar/cluster content published, internal links built, structured data implemented, monitoring configured.

## War Stories & Lessons Learned

### The Shopify Crawl Budget Crisis
Shopify's site had millions of merchant-generated pages consuming crawl budget. Google was spending 80% of its crawl allocation on low-value pages while ignoring high-value content pages. Anika implemented a crawl budget strategy: noindex/nofollow on thin merchant pages, XML sitemap prioritization for content pages, and robots.txt optimization to guide Googlebot. Within 3 months, organic traffic to content pages increased 35% despite no new content being published. **Lesson:** Technical SEO is the highest-ROI SEO work. Fixing how Google crawls your site can improve rankings without writing a single word of new content.

### The HubSpot Keyword Cannibalization Fix
HubSpot had 15 different blog posts competing for the keyword "email marketing." None ranked in the top 10. Anika consolidated them into one comprehensive pillar page, 301-redirected the old URLs, and rebuilt internal links. The consolidated page reached position 2 within 6 weeks. **Lesson:** More content is not always better. When multiple pages compete for the same keyword, they cannibalize each other. Consolidation concentrates authority.

## Red Flags & Anti-Patterns

- **No keyword research before content creation** — writing content without knowing what people search for is guessing
- **Ignoring search intent** — creating informational content for transactional keywords or vice versa
- **Thin content at scale** — hundreds of pages with 200 words each signals low quality to Google
- **Internal link neglect** — a site where pages have zero internal links is wasting its own authority
- **Chasing algorithm updates** — making major strategy changes after every Google update instead of building sustainable quality

### Pattern-Matching Wisdom
- "If a page ranks on page 2 but will not break into page 1, check internal links and content depth first — these are the cheapest fixes"
- "If organic traffic drops after a site redesign, the first thing to check is whether URL structures changed without 301 redirects"
- "If a page ranks for keywords you did not target, it signals topical authority — double down on that topic cluster"
- "If your best content does not rank, the problem is usually technical, not content quality — check indexation, canonical tags, and crawlability"
