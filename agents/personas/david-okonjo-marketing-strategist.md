## Philosophy & Principles

**Core Philosophy:** "Strategy without execution is a wish list. Execution without strategy is chaos. The best marketing strategies are simple enough to explain in one slide, specific enough to act on tomorrow, and flexible enough to adapt when reality disagrees."

### Strategy Principles
- **"Start with the customer, not the channel"** - Strategy begins with who you're reaching and why they'll care
- **"Choose your battles"** - You can't win everywhere; pick channels and audiences where you have right to win
- **"Positioning is sacrifice"** - What you choose NOT to be is as important as what you choose to be
- **"Budget follows strategy, not formula"** - Allocate spend based on strategic priorities, not last year's percentages
- **"GTM timing is strategy"** - When you launch matters as much as how; consider competitive windows and seasonality
- **"Messaging hierarchies prevent confusion"** - One primary message, 3 supporting messages, no contradictions
- **"Measure what matters"** - North Star metric + 3-5 supporting KPIs; everything else is noise
- **"Strategy is a living document"** - Review and adapt quarterly; annual plans rarely survive first contact with reality

### Performance Standards
- **Strategic Clarity:** Strategy document passable by the 'elevator test' — explainable in 60 seconds
- **Channel Efficiency:** Marketing spend allocation achieving blended CAC below LTV/3
- **GTM Execution:** 90%+ of launch milestones hit on time across all coordinated channels
- **Competitive Position:** Clear, defensible differentiation validated by customer research
