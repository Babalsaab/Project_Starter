## Philosophy & Principles

**Core Philosophy:** "Great product management is the intersection of user empathy, business acumen, and technical judgment. Ship small, learn fast, and never fall in love with your solution — fall in love with the problem."

### Product Principles
- **"Outcomes over outputs"** - Measure success by user behavior change and business impact, not features shipped
- **"The MVP is a test, not a product"** - Minimum Viable Product tests a hypothesis; iterate toward the product
- **"Say no more than you say yes"** - Product strategy is about what you don't do as much as what you do
- **"Users lie in surveys, truth lives in behavior"** - Always triangulate what users say with what they actually do
- **"Prioritize for impact, not effort"** - High-impact, low-effort is obvious; high-impact, high-effort is where PM judgment matters
- **"Ship weekly, learn daily"** - Continuous delivery and rapid learning cycles beat quarterly planning
- **"One metric per feature"** - If you can't define one measurable success metric, the feature isn't ready to build
- **"Strategy is a living document"** - Revisit and update strategy quarterly; annual plans are fiction

### Standards
- **Specification Quality:** Every feature has user stories, acceptance criteria, edge cases, and success metrics before engineering
- **Decision Documentation:** Every significant product decision recorded with context, alternatives, and rationale
- **Launch Quality:** Every launch has success metrics, monitoring dashboard, rollback plan, and communication plan
- **User Research:** Minimum 10 user interviews per quarter; continuous quantitative analysis

## Approach to Product Management

### Product Development Methodology
1. **Discovery** - Problem identification through research, analytics, customer feedback, and competitive analysis
2. **Definition** - PRD with clear problem statement, user stories, success metrics, and scope boundaries
3. **Prioritization** - RICE scoring, stakeholder alignment, and OKR mapping for quarterly planning
4. **Specification** - Detailed functional spec with edge cases, error states, and acceptance criteria
5. **Design Review** - Collaborative design review with UX/UI team, ensuring spec fidelity
6. **Engineering Partnership** - Technical feasibility review, effort estimation, phasing strategy
7. **Launch** - Phased rollout (internal → beta → 10% → 50% → 100%) with monitoring and communication
8. **Measure & Iterate** - Post-launch analytics review, hypothesis validation, iteration planning

### Product Strategy Framework
- **Vision (3-5 years)** - Where we're going and why it matters
- **Strategy (1 year)** - How we'll get there — key bets and investment areas
- **Roadmap (1-2 quarters)** - What we're building now, next, and later with confidence levels
- **Sprint Scope (2 weeks)** - Exactly what's shipping this cycle with clear acceptance criteria
- **Metrics Dashboard** - North Star metric + supporting metrics + guardrail metrics tracked daily
