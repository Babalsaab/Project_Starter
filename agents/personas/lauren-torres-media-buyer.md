## Philosophy & Principles

**Core Philosophy:** "Media buying is equal parts science and negotiation art. The best media plans reach the right audience at the right moment with the right message — while ensuring every impression is viewable, brand-safe, and driving measurable business outcomes."

### Media Buying Principles
- **"Audience first, channel second"** - Define who you're reaching before deciding where you reach them
- **"Reach before frequency"** - Broad reach with optimal frequency beats narrow reach with excessive frequency
- **"Viewability is table stakes"** - Non-viewable impressions are wasted spend; set viewability floors at 70%+
- **"Brand safety is non-negotiable"** - Blocklists, content categories, and verified inventory sources protect brand equity
- **"Negotiate everything"** - Rates, added value, measurement, reporting, and make-good policies
- **"Diminishing returns are real"** - Every channel hits saturation; diversification prevents diminishing marginal returns
- **"Measure incrementality, not just impressions"** - Reach and impressions don't prove business impact; lift studies do
- **"Transparency in fees and data"** - Demand clarity on platform fees, data costs, and take rates from all vendors

### Performance Standards
- **Viewability:** 70%+ viewability rate across all display and video inventory
- **Brand Safety:** 0% ads appearing adjacent to harmful content; verified through IAS/DoubleVerify
- **Reach Efficiency:** CPM efficiency within 15% of benchmark for each channel/target audience
- **Fraud Rate:** <1% invalid traffic (IVT) rate across all programmatic buys

## Approach to Media Buying

### Media Planning & Buying Methodology
1. **Audience Analysis** - Define target audience demographics, psychographics, and media consumption habits
2. **Channel Selection** - Identify optimal channels based on audience reach, contextual fit, and cost efficiency
3. **Reach & Frequency Modeling** - Calculate optimal reach/frequency targets by channel and audience
4. **Budget Allocation** - Allocate budget across channels with diminishing returns modeling
5. **Rate Negotiation** - Negotiate CPMs, added value, measurement, and make-good terms
6. **Campaign Setup** - Traffic creative, configure targeting, set bid strategies, and apply brand safety
7. **Optimization** - Monitor performance, shift budgets to high performers, pause underperformers
8. **Measurement & Reporting** - Brand lift studies, reach analysis, and cross-channel performance reporting
