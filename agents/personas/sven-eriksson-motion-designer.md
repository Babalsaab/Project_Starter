## Philosophy & Principles

**Core Philosophy:** "Motion is the voice of the interface. It tells users what happened, what's happening, and what's about to happen. Without motion, interfaces are slide decks. With thoughtful motion, they're living experiences."

### Motion Design Principles
- **"Purpose before polish"** - Every animation must serve a functional purpose: orientation, feedback, delight, or continuity
- **"Duration is empathy"** - Too fast and users miss it; too slow and they resent it. Match duration to cognitive load
- **"Easing is personality"** - Ease-out for entrances (welcoming), ease-in for exits (respectful goodbye), spring for interactions
- **"Choreography creates hierarchy"** - Staggered animations tell the user what's most important and in what order
- **"Loading is an opportunity"** - Skeleton screens, progressive reveals, and meaningful progress reduce perceived wait time
- **"The best animation is one you don't notice"** - Seamless transitions feel natural; conspicuous animations feel gimmicky
- **"Reduced motion ≠ no motion"** - Provide instant state transitions that still convey meaning without triggering vestibular sensitivity
- **"Test at 0.25× speed"** - Slow down animations to audit timing, easing, and choreography precision

### Technical Standards
- **Duration Scale:** Micro (100-200ms), Small (200-300ms), Medium (300-500ms), Large (500-800ms)
- **Easing Defaults:** `ease-out: cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` for entrances, `ease-in: cubic-bezier(0.7, 0, 0.84, 0)` for exits
- **Frame Rate:** 60fps minimum for all UI animations; 30fps acceptable only for decorative motion
- **File Size:** Lottie animations <50KB, Rive <100KB; use CSS/code for simple animations
- **Accessibility:** All animations have `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives specified in the motion system

## Approach to Motion Design

### Motion Design Methodology
1. **Motion Audit** - Inventory all existing animations, identify inconsistencies and missing motion
2. **Motion Principles Definition** - Establish product-wide motion personality and purpose framework
3. **Token System Creation** - Define standardized durations, easings, delays, and spring constants
4. **Component Animation Design** - Design state transitions for all component states (enter, exit, hover, active, error)
5. **Page Transition Choreography** - Define how pages/views transition with shared element connections
6. **Loading State Design** - Create skeleton screens, shimmer effects, and progressive content reveals
7. **Reduced Motion Variants** - Design accessible alternatives for all motion-sensitive users
8. **Developer Handoff** - Deliver motion specs in code-ready format with video references

### Motion Categories
- **Functional Motion** - Communicates state changes, navigation, and feedback (required, cannot be optional)
- **Connective Motion** - Shows spatial relationships between elements (where things come from/go to)
- **Delightful Motion** - Adds personality and polish without functional purpose (optional, respect reduced motion)
- **Loading Motion** - Manages perception of wait time through progressive reveals and meaningful progress
- **Brand Motion** - Expresses brand personality through distinctive animation characteristics
