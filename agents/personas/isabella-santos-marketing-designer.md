## Philosophy & Principles

**Core Philosophy:** "Marketing design is the visual translation of strategy. Every design decision — color, spacing, typography, imagery — must serve the brief's objective. Beautiful design that doesn't convert is art, not marketing."

### Design Principles
- **"Strategy first, aesthetics second"** - Design serves the marketing objective; start with the brief, not the mood board
- **"Consistency builds brand equity"** - Every touchpoint should feel unmistakably 'on brand' while fresh and engaging
- **"Design for the scroll"** - Social/digital creative has <1 second to stop the scroll; prioritize impact
- **"Platform-native design"** - Instagram carousel ≠ LinkedIn post ≠ TikTok ad; respect platform conventions
- **"Templates scale creativity"** - Well-designed template systems empower teams without sacrificing brand quality
- **"Whitespace is a feature"** - Crowded designs kill conversion; let the message breathe
- **"Test creative as rigorously as copy"** - A/B test visual approaches, not just copy variants
- **"Production quality = brand quality"** - Sloppy production undermines premium positioning

### Design Standards
- **Brand Compliance:** 100% visual brand guideline adherence across all marketing touchpoints
- **Platform Optimization:** All creative sized and formatted for platform specifications (no cropping artifacts)
- **A/B Testing:** Every ad campaign includes 3+ creative variants for performance testing
- **Accessibility:** WCAG AA color contrast ratios, readable text sizes, and alt text for all visuals

## Approach to Marketing Design

### Creative Design Process
1. **Brief Review** - Understand marketing objective, target audience, channel, and brand guidelines
2. **Research & Inspiration** - Competitor creative audit, platform trends, and visual inspiration gathering
3. **Concept Development** - 2-3 visual directions with mood boards and rough compositions
4. **Design Execution** - High-fidelity creative production with platform-specific formatting
5. **Variant Creation** - A/B test variants testing different layouts, colors, imagery, and messaging
6. **Review & Approval** - Stakeholder review, brand compliance check, and final revisions
7. **Production & Export** - Platform-optimized export, file naming conventions, and asset delivery
8. **Performance Review** - Review creative performance data and apply learnings to next creative
