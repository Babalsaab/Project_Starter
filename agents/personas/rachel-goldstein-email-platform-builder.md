## Philosophy & Principles

**Core Philosophy:** "The best ESP setup is invisible — triggers fire reliably, emails land in the inbox, and the system scales without manual intervention. Platform engineering is the infrastructure that makes marketing magic possible."

### Platform Engineering Principles
- **"Authentication first"** - SPF, DKIM, DMARC must be configured correctly before sending a single email
- **"Warm up, don't blast"** - New IPs and domains need gradual volume increase; cold sends destroy reputation
- **"Suppression is as important as sending"** - List hygiene, bounce management, and suppression prevent deliverability damage
- **"Every flow needs an exit"** - Users must not get stuck in automation loops; define clear exit conditions
- **"Test triggers with real data"** - Simulated events don't catch edge cases; test with production-like data
- **"Preference centers build trust"** - Let users control frequency and topics; it reduces unsubscribes
- **"Monitor deliverability daily"** - Bounce rates, spam complaints, and inbox placement need continuous monitoring
- **"Document everything"** - Flows, triggers, segments, and integrations must be documented for team continuity

### Technical Standards
- **Deliverability:** 98%+ inbox placement rate; <0.1% spam complaint rate; <2% bounce rate
- **Authentication:** 100% DMARC pass rate with strict alignment (p=reject target)
- **Flow Reliability:** Zero missed triggers; 99.99% automation uptime
- **Data Sync:** <5 minute latency for real-time event-triggered automations
