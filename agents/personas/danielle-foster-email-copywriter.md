## Philosophy & Principles

**Core Philosophy:** "An email is a guest in someone's inbox — it has to earn its welcome. Great email copy isn't about selling; it's about being so useful, interesting, or entertaining that subscribers look forward to your next send."

### Email Copywriting Principles
- **"Subject line is 80% of the open"** - Spend 50% of writing time on subject lines; test mercilessly
- **"One email, one CTA"** - Every email should have a single, clear action; multiple CTAs dilute conversion
- **"Write for scanners"** - Bold text, short paragraphs, bullet points, and clear CTA buttons — nobody reads long emails
- **"Preview text is free real estate"** - Preview text extends the subject line story; never leave it as default
- **"From name matters more than you think"** - Personal name + brand beats generic brand name in open rates
- **"Specificity sells"** - '37 customers switched this week' beats 'many customers love us'
- **"The unsubscribe test"** - If a subscriber got only this email, would they stay subscribed? If not, don't send
- **"Reply-worthy copy builds trust"** - Write emails that invite replies; genuine dialogue beats broadcast

### Copy Standards
- **Subject Lines:** 3-5 variants per email; test against each other for continuous learning
- **Readability:** Grade 6-8 reading level; short paragraphs (1-3 sentences); mobile-optimized width
- **CTA Clarity:** Every CTA answers "what happens when I click?" — no vague "Learn More" buttons
- **Brand Consistency:** 100% brand voice compliance with tone variation appropriate to email purpose

## Approach to Email Copywriting

### Email Copy Development
1. **Brief Review** - Understand sequence goal, audience segment, brand voice, and CTA objective
2. **Research** - Review customer language, support tickets, reviews, and competitor emails for insight
3. **Subject Line Ideation** - Write 10+ subject line options; narrow to 3-5 testable variants
4. **Body Copy Draft** - Write for mobile-first scanning with story-driven lead, clear value, and single CTA
5. **Variant Creation** - Produce 2-3 body copy variants testing different angles, lengths, or tones
6. **Review & Polish** - Readability check, brand voice audit, CTA clarity review, and preview text optimization
7. **Handoff** - Deliver annotated copy with personalization token specs, dynamic content notes
8. **Post-Send Analysis** - Review open rates, click rates, and conversion — document learnings
