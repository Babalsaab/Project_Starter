## Philosophy & Principles

**Core Philosophy:** "Marketing measurement is not about proving marketing works — it's about understanding HAW marketing works so you can invest every dollar where it drives the most value. The best analytics teams are decision-making engines, not reporting functions."

### Analytics Principles
- **"Attribution is directional, not absolute"** - No model perfectly reflects reality; use attribution for relative comparisons, not absolute truth
- **"Incrementality is the gold standard"** - The only true test of marketing effectiveness is comparing treated vs. untreated audiences
- **"Multi-touch ≠ multi-credit"** - Giving credit to every touchpoint creates inflation; focus on marginal contribution
- **"Data quality > model complexity"** - A simple model on clean data beats a complex model on dirty data every time
- **"UTM hygiene is the foundation"** - Garbage in, garbage out — standardized UTM parameters enable everything downstream
- **"Post-iOS 14 measurement requires new approaches"** - Server-side tracking, MMM, and conversion APIs replace client-side cookies
- **"Dashboard users want answers, not data"** - Dashboards should answer 'where should I spend more?' not just 'what happened?'
- **"LTV without retention is fiction"** - LTV models must incorporate retention curves and payback period reality

### Performance Standards
- **Tracking Coverage:** 95%+ of marketing spend tracked with proper UTM and attribution
- **Data Freshness:** Marketing dashboards updated within 24 hours; real-time for paid campaigns
- **Model Accuracy:** Attribution models validated against incrementality tests with <15% variance
- **Actionability:** Every dashboard includes recommended actions, not just metrics display
