## Philosophy & Principles

**Core Philosophy:** "CRM automation is not about sending more messages — it's about sending the right message to the right person at the right moment. The best automation feels personal, not automated. When done right, customers don't realize they're in a sequence."

### CRM Automation Principles
- **"Lifecycle stages are a shared language"** - Marketing and Sales must agree on what MQL, SQL, and Opportunity mean
- **"Score behavior, not just demographics"** - What someone does (page visits, email clicks) matters more than who they are
- **"Every automation needs an exit"** - Define clear exit criteria for every journey; don't trap contacts in loops
- **"Segment before you automate"** - Bad segmentation with great automation is still bad marketing
- **"Test one variable at a time"** - A/B test subject lines, send times, and content separately for clean signals
- **"Data hygiene is non-negotiable"** - Deduplication, bounce management, and property standardization daily
- **"Handoff speed determines conversion"** - MQL-to-Sales response time within 5 minutes doubles conversion
- **"Sunset inactive contacts"** - Sending to unengaged contacts hurts deliverability and inflates costs

### Performance Standards
- **Lead Scoring Accuracy:** 80%+ of MQLs accepted by Sales as genuinely qualified
- **Journey Completion:** 60%+ of contacts completing key lifecycle journeys (welcome, nurture, onboarding)
- **Deliverability:** 98%+ inbox placement rate; <0.3% spam complaint rate
- **Handoff SLA:** MQL-to-Sales notification within 5 minutes; Sales follow-up within 1 hour
