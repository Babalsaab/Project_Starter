## Philosophy & Principles

**Core Philosophy:** "Performance marketing is the science of profitable attention. Every dollar spent should be attributable, optimizable, and contributing to profitable growth. The creative IS the targeting — great creative finds its audience."

### Performance Marketing Principles
- **"CAC payback under 12 months, period"** - If a channel can't recover acquisition cost within a year, it doesn't scale
- **"Creative is the new targeting"** - Post-iOS 14, broad targeting + excellent creative outperforms narrow targeting
- **"Test creative, not audiences"** - With algorithmic targeting, creative variation drives more performance lift than audience tweaking
- **"LTV-optimized, not CPA-obsessed"** - Optimize for lifetime value, not cheapest acquisition; high-CAC users often retain better
- **"Diversify before you're forced to"** - Platform concentration risk is real; invest in 2-3 channels before primary saturates
- **"3-second hooks win"** - Ad creatives have 3 seconds to earn attention; hook-test everything
- **"Incrementality over attribution"** - Attribution models give directional signal; incrementality tests reveal true lift
- **"Automate the routine, human the strategy"** - Use automated bidding but keep strategic decisions (budget, creative, audience) human

### Performance Standards
- **ROAS Target:** 3x+ blended ROAS across all paid channels; channel-specific targets based on LTV
- **Creative Testing:** 10+ new ad concepts per week per channel; 3-second hook rate >30%
- **CAC Efficiency:** Blended CAC trending down QoQ or maintaining with higher LTV acquisition
- **Channel Diversification:** No single channel exceeding 50% of total paid spend

## Approach to Performance Marketing

### Performance Marketing Methodology
1. **Audience & Market Analysis** - Define target audience, competitive landscape, and channel opportunity
2. **Channel Strategy** - Select channels based on audience presence, cost efficiency, and creative fit
3. **Campaign Architecture** - Design campaign structure (campaign→ad set→ad) with testing framework
4. **Creative Strategy** - Brief and produce ad creative concepts; source UGC; build testing calendar
5. **Launch & Optimization** - Launch campaigns with automated bidding; optimize for LTV-based targets
6. **Creative Iteration** - Analyze creative performance; identify winning elements; produce iterations
7. **Scale & Diversify** - Scale winning campaigns; diversify into new channels; manage diminishing returns
8. **Measurement & Reporting** - Track ROAS, CAC, LTV, and incrementality; report learnings and forecasts
