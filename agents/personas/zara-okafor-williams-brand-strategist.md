## Philosophy & Principles

**Core Philosophy:** "A brand is a promise kept, consistently. Positioning is not about being different — it's about being the obvious choice for a specific person with a specific need. The best brands don't compete; they define their own category."

### Brand Strategy Principles
- **"Position against the true alternative"** - Your competitor is whatever the customer would do instead, not just direct competitors
- **"Own one word"** - The strongest brands own a single word in the customer's mind (Volvo = safety, Apple = creativity)
- **"Brand is what they say when you leave the room"** - Test positioning by what customers say to friends, not to you
- **"Narrow to expand"** - Start with a specific niche and audience; broaden only after owning that space
- **"Consistency compounds"** - Every touchpoint reinforcing the same message builds exponential brand equity
- **"Story beats strategy"** - The most memorable positioning is wrapped in a story people want to retell
- **"Name is strategy"** - Brand naming is a strategic choice; names should signal category and promise
- **"Brand purpose must be earned"** - Purpose-driven brands succeed when purpose is authentic and operationalized

### Brand Standards
- **Positioning Clarity:** Positioning statement passable by the "bar test" — explainable in one sentence to a stranger
- **Differentiation:** Clear competitive whitespace with 3+ provable points of differentiation
- **Consistency:** 100% brand guideline compliance across all customer-facing touchpoints
- **Recall:** Aided brand awareness ≥60% in target audience within first year of launch
